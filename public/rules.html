Community Rules

Last updated: [DATE]

MeetDoConnect is for people who want a good evening without alcohol and want to find people to do things with. These rules keep every room friendly, clean and safe. Agreeing to them is part of joining.

1. Keep it kind and clean

No nudity, sexual content, harassment, hate, threats or bullying. Treat everyone in the room the way you would like to be treated.

2. Respect privacy

  • Do not record, screenshot or share anyone else's image, voice or words.
  • Members are shown by first name only. Do not share personal details about another member.

3. No selling or recruiting

Do not advertise, sell or recruit during sessions or in messages to other members.

4. Sessions are alcohol-free

Please do not drink alcohol during a session. Everything on the supply cards is made without it.

5. Adults hold the account

Accounts are for adults. Children may join family sessions on a household account, with an adult in the room the whole time.

6. Cameras and sound

Cameras are optional. Mute your microphone when you are not talking. Quiet sessions have no talking at all.

7. Activity buddies without pressure

  • After a session you may pick people you would do it again with. Picks are private. A buddy chat opens only when two people pick each other.
  • MeetDoConnect is for friendship first. Do not pressure anyone. If someone says no or goes quiet, leave it there.
  • Do not contact members who did not pick you. A buddy can unmatch or block you at any time.

8. Safety tools and what happens next

Report, Mute and Leave are always on screen in every room.

  • When you report someone, they are removed from the room right away and the account is flagged.
  • Two reports from different members within 30 days suspends the account until we review it.
  • We review the report queue every week. Serious breaches end the membership.
  • If you think a decision was wrong, write to [EMAIL].

9. Not an emergency service

MeetDoConnect is not a crisis or medical service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number. Session content is general wellness and activity guidance, not medical advice or therapy.
