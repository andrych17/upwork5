Domain

Moving the domain from GoDaddy to Cloudflare

  1. Add it to Cloudflare

    Free plan. Cloudflare copies the DNS records it finds, and I check the email (MX) records came across so your mail keeps working.

  2. Swap the nameservers

    At GoDaddy, turn off DNSSEC if it is on, then enter the two nameservers Cloudflare gives you.

  3. Wait for "Active"

    Usually under an hour. GoDaddy says up to 48 hours, and nothing on the old setup goes down meanwhile.

  4. Attach to Pages

    The bare domain and www both point at the Pages project. Cloudflare issues the HTTPS certificate on its own.

The registration itself can stay at GoDaddy. Moving it to Cloudflare Registrar is optional: it needs the GoDaddy auth code, adds a year of renewal at cost, and isn't allowed in the first 60 days after a domain is registered or transferred.
