A room that runs itself

Social sessions split into rooms of about 5. A shared timer, prompts that appear at the right moment, and rotating roles do the leading. Quiet sessions have no talking and need no structure beyond a check-in and check-out.

Friendship first

Find activity buddies

After a session, tap who you would do it again with. Nobody sees who you tapped. If two people tap each other, a private buddy chat opens. Dating can happen on its own, with no pressure and no label.

Who would you do this again with?

In this demo, Priya and Jo have already picked you. Marcus and Dana have not.

  • Only first names, and only people you shared a session with.
  • One-sided picks stay private. Neither person is told.
  • Either buddy can unmatch or block at any time.
  • MeetDoConnect never shares contact details. Buddies decide whether to swap them.

Questions

Is this a sobriety program?

No. It is for anyone who wants a good evening without alcohol, whether they drink sometimes or never. It is not treatment or recovery support.

Do I need to be creative or on camera?

No. Most sessions walk you through each step, and cameras are optional.

What do I need to buy?

The prep card lists supplies, mostly kitchen and craft-drawer items, with an estimated cost.

Can kids join?

Kitchen-Table Lab, Mocktail Studio and Cards and Collage Night are made for families. An adult books and stays in the room.

Are sessions recorded?

No, so people can relax and take part.
