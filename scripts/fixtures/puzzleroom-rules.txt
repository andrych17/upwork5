PUZZLE ROOM - RULES
For 2 to 12 players, in teams of 1 to 6. Puzzles are talked over out loud in the video room. Almost no typing: every puzzle has four answers to tap.

OBJECT OF THE GAME
Work with your team to solve riddles and word puzzles together, out loud, before the clock runs out.

HOW TO WIN
Score the most points. A solved puzzle is worth 10 points, plus up to 5 more for speed, minus 3 for each hint you use and 2 for each wrong answer. An unsolved puzzle scores 0. Your total is every puzzle added up. When the session ends, the highest total wins. Teams tied on the top total win together.

THE RULES
1. Players are put into teams when the game starts, either at random or by choosing (set in the lobby before Start). With 2 players each plays alone as a team of one; with 3 players it is a team of 2 against a team of 1.
2. Teams take the stage one at a time. First comes a 10-second huddle: the team is shown 3 puzzle categories and each teammate taps one (most taps wins; ties and no taps are settled at random).
3. Then everyone sees one puzzle with four possible answers. The team on stage has 90 seconds. Talk it over OUT LOUD with your team: only your team's Reader (a different teammate each round) can tap. Everyone else watches and may chat quietly, but please don't call out answers.
4. The Reader can tap Hint (up to 2 times) or Give up. A wrong tap crosses that answer out.
5. Scoring for a solved puzzle: 10 points, minus 3 per hint, minus 2 per wrong tap (never below 1), plus a speed bonus of up to 5 points for the time left. An unsolved puzzle (gave up or out of time) scores 0.
6. After each puzzle the answer is shown for a few seconds. After every team has had a turn, the round ends and the scoreboard updates. Then there is a 60-second chat break with a conversation prompt (anyone can end it early), and a new round begins.
7. Rounds keep going until there is not enough time left for another chat break and a full round. The highest total wins; teams tied on the top total win together.
8. No round starts unless it can finish before the 60-minute session ends. If time does run out, the team on stage is not scored.
