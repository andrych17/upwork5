DRAW AND GUESS - RULES
For 2 to 12 players, everyone for themselves. Everything is spoken out loud in the video room. No typing.

OBJECT OF THE GAME
One player draws a secret word on their phone while everyone else watches the picture appear and shouts out guesses, out loud. Take turns drawing so everyone gets a go.

HOW TO WIN
Score the most points. Guess fast for 5, 3 or 2 points, and earn 2 points as the artist for every player who guesses your drawing. When the session ends, the highest total wins. Players tied on the top total win together.

THE RULES
1. 2 to 12 players can play, everyone for themselves. A round is every player drawing once, in a random order.
2. The artist is shown 3 words and picks one (or asks for 3 new words, once). Nobody else sees the word. A word is picked for the artist if the 12 seconds run out.
3. The artist draws on their phone for 60 seconds. No letters, numbers, talking or pointing! Everyone else watches the drawing appear and guesses OUT LOUD. No typing. The artist has six colors, three line widths, an Eraser to rub out part of the picture, Undo for the last line, and Clear to wipe the whole drawing and start over.
4. When someone says the right answer, the artist taps that player's name. A guesser earns 5 points in the first third of the time, 3 in the second third and 2 in the last third. The artist earns 2 points for every player who guesses.
5. The turn ends when everyone has guessed, the artist ends it, or the time runs out. The word is shown to everyone.
6. After everyone has drawn once, there is a 60-second chat break with a conversation prompt (anyone can end it early), and a new round begins.
7. Rounds keep going while a chat break and a full round fit before the 60-minute session ends, so everyone draws the same number of times. The highest total wins; players tied on the top total win together.
