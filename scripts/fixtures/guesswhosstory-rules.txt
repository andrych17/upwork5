GUESS WHO'S STORY - RULES
For 2 to 12 players, everyone for themselves. Talked over out loud in the video room. No typing: players only tap Yes or No, then tap a name.

OBJECT OF THE GAME
Find out who in the room said yes to a question about themselves, by talking it over out loud, and hear the story behind it.

HOW TO WIN
Score the most points. Guess the mystery person for 3 points. When you are the mystery person, you score 1 point, plus 1 more for every player who guesses wrong (up to 5 extra). When the session ends, the highest score wins. Players tied on the top score win together.

THE RULES
1. A round is 6 questions long.
2. A question about you appears, like "Have you ever ridden a horse?" Everyone secretly taps Yes or No (8 seconds).
3. With 3 or more players, the game picks ONE of the people who said Yes as the mystery person (people who have been picked the fewest times go first). Everyone sees how many players said Yes.
4. Talk it over OUT LOUD for 40 seconds: who do you think it is, and why? Ask questions, joke around, trade clues. The mystery person tries to stay cool. Anyone can tap "Ready to guess" to move on early.
5. Then everyone except the mystery person taps who they think it is (12 seconds). A right guess is 3 points. The mystery person gets 1 point for being picked, plus 1 for every wrong guess (up to 5).
6. STORY TIME: the mystery person is revealed and tells the story behind their Yes out loud (up to 30 seconds; anyone can tap Next to move on). Players who said Yes but were not picked are revealed too.
7. With exactly 2 players, you both tap Yes or No in secret, talk, then each guess what the OTHER person answered. A right guess is 3 points, and you get 1 point whenever the other person guesses wrong. Then you both share the story.
8. If nobody says Yes, the question is skipped.
9. After 6 questions there are round results, then a 60-second chat break with a conversation prompt (anyone can end it early), and the next round begins.
10. Rounds keep going while a full round fits before the 60-minute session ends. If time runs out in the middle of a question, that question is not scored.
